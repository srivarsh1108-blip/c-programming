ASCII - American Standard Code for Information interchange

Every keys has an Ascii value. Why?

Machine Can understand only 0 or 1

There are 256 ascii code

0 - Null
1 to 31 - Non printable characters (ctrl , alt, arrows, etc ...)
32 - Spacebar
65 to 90 - Capital
97 to 122 - Small 
48 to 57- Numeric
33 to 47 , 58 to 64 , 91 to 96 , 123 to 127 - Symbols
From 128 reserved for extended ascii value
----------------------------------------------
